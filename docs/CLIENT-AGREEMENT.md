## About Tootica

Tootica is a dental practice management system that brings appointment scheduling, patient records, doctor and staff management, and practice analytics into one platform. Designed for dental clinics with one or more branches, it helps administrators, dentists and receptionists coordinate daily operations, manage access according to their roles and maintain a clear view of practice activity.

## Hardware and software requirements

For the hosted application, the following is the recommended minimum setup for each clinic workstation:

- **Processor and memory:** A dual-core processor with 4 GB RAM. For smoother use with multiple browser tabs or other applications, 8 GB RAM is recommended.

- **Operating system:** Windows 11, or a currently supported version of macOS or Linux capable of running the latest Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.

- **Display:** A desktop or laptop display with a resolution of at least 1366 x 768 pixels.

- **Browser:** The latest stable version of Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge, with JavaScript and cookies enabled.

- **Internet:** A stable broadband connection with at least 5 Mbps download and 1 Mbps upload available per active workstation. Larger file uploads may require more bandwidth. Your network must allow HTTPS access to Tootica; offline use is not supported.

- **Additional equipment:** Printers and scanners are optional unless required for your agreed workflow. Confirm compatibility with Tootica before purchasing or connecting them.

No local server or database installation is required. These are recommended starting specifications; suitability should be confirmed during onboarding using your clinic's devices, network and expected workload.

## Installation and training terms

Tootica will coordinate application setup and training with your clinic based on an agreed scope and schedule.

- **Readiness:** Ensure the required devices, internet access and authorised team members are available for setup and training.

- **Setup:** Confirm the configuration, data preparation, migration and third-party integrations included in the agreed scope.

- **Training:** Online training is included. In-person training may be provided subject to availability and scheduling agreed with Tootica. Instructional videos will also be shared to help your team use the system.

- **Scheduling:** Agree the training topics, participants and session dates with Tootica. Inform us promptly of any changes in availability or technical readiness.

After setup and training, review any outstanding items with Tootica. Additional work outside the agreed scope will be discussed and agreed separately.

## Support and escalation terms

Use the support contact details and channels provided for your subscription. Support hours, included services, response targets and escalation contacts should be confirmed in your agreed support arrangement.

When raising a request, include your organisation’s name, a contact person, a clear description of the issue, its effect on your work and any relevant error message. Where helpful, include screenshots with unnecessary confidential information removed.

- **Initial request:** Describe the issue and any steps already taken so the support team can assess it.

- **Follow-up:** Use the existing ticket or reference number when providing further information or requesting an update.

- **Escalation:** If the issue remains unresolved or its business impact increases, request escalation by emailing support@tootica.om and explain the updated impact. If urgent resolution is required, a Tootica consultant will contact you to assess the issue and coordinate the next steps.

- **Coordination:** Provide an authorised contact for any troubleshooting session and agree on access arrangements before remote assistance.

Issues involving third-party services, equipment or changes outside the agreed scope may require additional coordination. Confirm any additional work and charges before proceeding.
